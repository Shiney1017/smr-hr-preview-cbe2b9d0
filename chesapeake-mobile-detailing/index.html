What Chesapeake is hard on

Chesapeake mixes farm roads and big commuter corridors. Cars that run I-64, I-464 and the Chesapeake Expressway pick up road film and brake dust fast, and trucks out by Hickory and Fentress come home with field dust and red clay in the wheel wells. Spring brings heavy yellow pine pollen from the Great Dismal Swamp side of the city, and summer humidity bakes bird droppings and tree sap into clear coat within days.

Local tip: In spring, rinse pollen off before it sits in the rain; a sealant or ceramic coating makes that a two-minute job. After a run down the Dismal Swamp Canal Trail area or a farm road, get the undercarriage and wheel wells cleaned before the clay dries.
