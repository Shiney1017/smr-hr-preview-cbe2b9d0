What York County & Poquoson is hard on

Yorktown sits on the York River, where river and bay air carry salt inland. Heavily wooded neighborhoods in Tabb and Grafton coat cars in pollen each spring, and Route 17 and the Coleman Bridge put commuters on long, exposed stretches over the water. Few places in Hampton Roads sit lower or closer to salt water. Marsh air, frequent tidal flooding and boat ramps mean salt reaches paint, wheels and underbodies on a daily basis, and towing to Messick Point puts extra salt and grime on trucks and trailers.

Local tip: Spring pollen plus rain leaves a yellow film that can stain light paint. Regular exterior details or a coating keep it from bonding. Rinse your tow vehicle and trailer after every ramp trip, and get the underbody cleaned regularly. Ceramic coating helps paint shed salt film.
