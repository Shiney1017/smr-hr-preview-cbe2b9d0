What Hampton is hard on

Hampton faces the Chesapeake Bay and the mouth of the James, so Buckroe, Fox Hill and Fort Monroe cars live in salt air. Daily trips through the Hampton Roads Bridge-Tunnel add tunnel grime and stop-and-go brake dust, and low streets in Fox Hill and Wythe can flood at high tide.

Local tip: After the HRBT backs up on a rainy day, wash brake dust off wheels before it bakes on. Ceramic-coated wheels clean up much faster.
